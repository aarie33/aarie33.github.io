## Context
A new-student admissions platform for Politeknik Negeri Jember, one of
several systems built for this institution, handling registration for
incoming students end to end.

## Technical challenge
Registration workflows have hard deadlines and zero tolerance for lost
submissions — the system had to be reliable under the admission-period
traffic spike, not just functionally complete.

## Key features
- Student data management
- New student registration
