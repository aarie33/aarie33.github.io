## Context
Built for Koperasi Kemenag Jember, a cooperative under the local Ministry
of Religious Affairs office, to run day-to-day point-of-sale operations.

## Technical challenge
A Laravel + Nuxt pairing rather than the more common Inertia setup used
elsewhere — chosen for a PoS UI that benefited from Nuxt's client-side
interactivity for fast checkout flows.

## Key features
- Product management
- Purchasing and sales transactions
- Sales and inventory reporting
