## Context
Irripedia is a field-survey tool built for Daya Santosa Rekayasa, an
irrigation engineering firm, to digitize land surveys that were previously
recorded on paper in the field.

## Technical challenge
Map-based data capture — marking survey locations, computing area and
elevation from field input, and summarizing saved locations on a map view.

## Key features
- Land survey: location marking, area and elevation calculation, data entry
- Map view: summary of saved survey locations
