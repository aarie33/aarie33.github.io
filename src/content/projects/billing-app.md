## Context
Built for Energasindo Heksa Karya, an energy distribution company, to
calculate and manage customer billing for natural gas usage.

## Technical challenge
Integrating Azure SSO for enterprise authentication and getting usage-based
billing calculations right — the kind of domain logic that has zero
tolerance for rounding or unit-conversion mistakes.

## Key features
- Azure SSO
- Billing calculation for natural gas usage
