## Context
E-Proposal digitizes proposal submission and review for Kementerian Desa,
PDT dan Transmigrasi (the Ministry of Villages, Disadvantaged Regions and
Transmigration) — replacing a paper-based funding-proposal workflow used
across many village-level applicants.

## Technical challenge
Modeling a multi-stage review/approval workflow so proposals moved through
the right approval chain without manual tracking, on Laravel + Inertia.

## Contribution
Led the build as Dev Lead, covering the workflow engine and the
applicant-facing submission flow.
