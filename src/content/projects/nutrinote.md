## Context
Nutrinote is a personal nutrition-logging tool, the more consumer-facing
counterpart to the institutional nutrition platforms built in the same
period.

## Technical challenge
Keeping day-to-day food-logging fast and low-friction on a server-rendered
Inertia stack, rather than defaulting to a heavier SPA.
